**WASHINGTON — June 12, 2026 —** The U.S. Department of War today published
the third release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous
Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting
System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). The collection continues to be housed on
WAR.GOV/UFO, and the Department says it will release additional files on a
rolling basis.

Chief Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell, in a statement accompanying the
release, said WAR.GOV/UFO has received "over 1.7 billion hits worldwide" since
the site's launch on May 8, 2026.

### What is in Release 03

The third tranche includes:

- **53 documents** drawn from the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), the
  Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), the National Aeronautics and Space
  Administration (NASA), the Department of Defense, and other unspecified
  agencies.
- **10 images**, including a set of clips and images that the Department
  describes as "artistic interpretations" of reported sightings.
- **Six new videos**, several of which depict orb-like objects.
- **Three NASA audio recordings**, including over two hours of recorded
  mission-debrief audio from the Apollo 16 mission (1972) and an excerpt
  from a November 1962 interview in which journalist Walter Cronkite asks
  NASA astronaut Gordon Cooper about his views on UFOs.

The release also bundles a new report dated **June 5, 2026, signed by Dr.
Jon T. Kosloski**, Director of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office
(AARO). The report notes one case involving an orange "mother" orb that
appears to launch smaller red orbs, and states that approximately **40
percent** of recently reported UAP cases "lack a reasonable explanation and
remain unresolved."

### Recurring "orb" theme

Multiple documents detail encounters reported by federal law-enforcement
officers in 2023, in which five agents reported observing strange orbs on
the horizon. The orb-shape category has been repeatedly identified by
military personnel and AARO officials in recent reporting cycles.

### PURSUE context

PURSUE — the **Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP
Encounters** — was launched on May 8, 2026. Release 01 (May 8) consisted of
roughly 162 photos, videos, and documents; Release 02 (May 22) was the first
to bundle records from CIA, the Office of the Director of National
Intelligence (ODNI), NASA, and the Department of Energy alongside DoW
holdings, including video of a four-UAP formation reported over Iran, a
Syrian UAP exhibiting "instant acceleration," the U.S. Air Force / Air
National Guard F-16C shoot-down of an object over Lake Huron on February 12,
2023, and a December 2019 East Coast case.

**华盛顿，2026 年 6 月 12 日电** —— 美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）
今日发布**第三批**已解密的历史性"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous
Phenomena, UAP）档案，作为"总统不明异常现象事件解封与报告系统"
（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE）
的一部分。整套档案集仍托管于 WAR.GOV/UFO 网站，战争部表示后续文件将
"以滚动方式持续发布"。

五角大楼首席发言人 **Sean Parnell** 在配发的声明中称，WAR.GOV/UFO 自
2026 年 5 月 8 日上线以来，已累计收到**超过 17 亿次**全球访问。

### Release 03 包含哪些内容

第三批材料包括：

- **53 份文件**，来自中央情报局（CIA）、联邦调查局（FBI）、美国国家航空航天局
  （NASA）、国防部以及其他未具名机构。
- **10 张图像**，其中一组图片和片段被战争部描述为对相关目击事件的
  "艺术性诠释"（artistic interpretations）。
- **6 段新视频**，其中数段呈现球状（orb-like）物体。
- **3 段 NASA 音频**，包括 1972 年阿波罗 16 号（Apollo 16）任务超过两小时
  的任务汇报录音，以及 1962 年 11 月记者 Walter Cronkite 就 UFO 议题
  采访 NASA 宇航员 Gordon Cooper 的片段。

本次发布还附带一份**日期为 2026 年 6 月 5 日、由全域异常解析办公室
（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）主任 Dr. Jon T. Kosloski 签署**
的报告。报告提到一例橙色"母体"球状物（"mother" orb）疑似释放出多个较小红色
球体的案例，并指出近期上报的 UAP 案例中约有 **40%**「缺乏合理解释、仍未结案」。

### 反复出现的"球体"主题

多份文件详述了 2023 年联邦执法人员上报的目击事件 —— 共有 5 名执法人员报告
在地平线上看到奇怪的球状物。球状形态在近年来军方人员与 AARO 官员的报告
循环中已被反复识别。

### PURSUE 背景

**PURSUE**（总统不明异常现象事件解封与报告系统）于 2026 年 5 月 8 日启动。
Release 01（5 月 8 日）包含约 162 份照片、视频和文件；Release 02（5 月 22 日）
首次将 CIA、国家情报总监办公室（ODNI）、NASA 与能源部（Department of Energy）
的档案与战争部档案合并发布，其中包括据报道在伊朗上空观测到的 4 个 UAP 编队
飞行视频、一段在叙利亚出现"瞬时加速"的 UAP 视频、2023 年 2 月 12 日美国
空军/空军国民警卫队 F-16C 在休伦湖（Lake Huron）击落一个物体的画面，以及
2019 年 12 月东海岸案例。
