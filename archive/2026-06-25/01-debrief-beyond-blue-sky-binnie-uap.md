## Original (English)

> Reconstructed from public search excerpts of the article and from NewsNation's companion piece ["Brian Binnie's UAP encounter is hard to dismiss, director says"](https://www.newsnationnow.com/space/brian-binnie-uap-account/). Not a verbatim transcript of The Debrief story.

A newly released documentary film, *Beyond Blue Sky: The Untold Story of the First Private Astronauts*, now available on Apple TV and Amazon Prime, revisits the team behind SpaceShipOne — the first privately built craft to cross the Kármán line — and, for the first time on screen, details a life-altering Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) encounter reported by one of its pilots, U.S. Navy combat veteran Brian Binnie.

The film follows the "renegade geniuses" who flew SpaceShipOne to space and back three times, winning both the Collier Trophy and the $10 million Ansari XPRIZE for a fraction of the cost of a NASA-class program. Binnie, a Princeton-educated aeronautical engineer and 20-year Navy aviator, was one of the two record-setting astronauts at the center of that effort.

According to the documentary, six days after SpaceShipOne's first powered flight test in December 2003 — and not long after a crushing crash-landing setback that nearly ended his program — Binnie saw roughly a dozen unidentified objects in his backyard. He watched the encounter for over a minute. Director Kevin Curran says Binnie described the event as something that "shook him to his core" and that he later credited with inspiring him to keep pushing through to the historic XPRIZE-winning flight.

Curran, interviewed in The Debrief and in NewsNation's *Reality Check* podcast, defends Binnie's credibility as "a highly trained observer" — a Navy combat pilot and test pilot — whose account "is hard to dismiss." Binnie had only confided the experience to a handful of aerospace colleagues for years; the documentary marks the first time the story was put on the record, and he died before the film's release.

The Debrief notes that Binnie agreed to put the story on camera in 2020, well before the high-profile House Oversight Committee whistleblower hearings of 2023 and before the wave of Pentagon UAP file releases in May–June 2026. The filmmaker calls Binnie "really brave to come out" with the account at a time when discussing UAP publicly still carried significant career and reputational risk for a working aerospace professional.

## 简体中文译稿

> 本段由公开搜索摘要与 NewsNation 配套报道（["Brian Binnie 的 UAP 目击难以否认"](https://www.newsnationnow.com/space/brian-binnie-uap-account/)）重建而成，非 The Debrief 原文逐字。

一部新近上线的纪录片《超越蓝天：首批商业宇航员不为人知的故事》（*Beyond Blue Sky: The Untold Story of the First Private Astronauts*）现已在 Apple TV 和 Amazon Prime 上线。影片回顾了打造"太空船一号"（SpaceShipOne）——首架由民间团队打造、成功跨越卡门线（Kármán line）的飞行器——的团队，并首次在银幕上披露其中一位飞行员、美国海军作战老兵布赖恩·宾尼（Brian Binnie）所亲历的一次"改变人生"的不明异常现象（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）目击事件。

影片记录了这群被称为"叛逆天才"（renegade geniuses）的工程师，如何驾驶"太空船一号"三度往返太空与地面，最终摘得 Collier 奖与 1000 万美元的 Ansari XPRIZE 大奖，且总成本只相当于 NASA 同级项目的一小部分。宾尼是普林斯顿大学（Princeton）航空工程师出身、服役 20 年的海军飞行员，是这一壮举的两位创纪录飞行员之一。

按纪录片的叙述：在 2003 年 12 月"太空船一号"首次动力飞行测试六天之后，也正当一次几乎让他出局的迫降事故余波未平之际，宾尼在自家后院看到了大约一打数量不明的不明物体（unidentified objects），并连续观察一分多钟。导演凯文·柯伦（Kevin Curran）转述称，宾尼形容那次经历"动摇了他的根基"（shook him to his core），他后来把这次经历归功于自己得以撑过低谷、并完成最终那次摘下 XPRIZE 大奖的历史性飞行的关键动力。

柯伦在接受 The Debrief 与 NewsNation 旗下 *Reality Check* 播客采访时，为宾尼的可信度辩护，称他是一位"训练有素的观察者"（highly trained observer）——既是海军作战飞行员，也是试飞员——他的陈述"很难被一笔带过"。多年里，宾尼只对极少数航空业内同事提过此事；这部纪录片是他首次将经历公开记录在案，而他在影片公映前已经离世。

The Debrief 指出，宾尼是在 2020 年同意将这段经历搬上镜头的——彼时距离 2023 年那场广受关注的众议院监督委员会（House Oversight Committee）举报人听证会还有数年，距离 2026 年 5—6 月那一波五角大楼 UAP 文件解密发布也尚有时日。纪录片制作方称宾尼"非常勇敢地站出来"（really brave to come out）——在公开谈论 UAP 仍可能让一名在职航空工程师付出职业与名誉代价的年代。
