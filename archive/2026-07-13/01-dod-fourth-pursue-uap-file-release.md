## Original (English)

### Headline

**Department of War Publishes Fourth Release of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Files on WAR.GOV/UFO**

Source: U.S. Department of War — Release dated July 10, 2026.

### Summary (synthesized from cross-corroborated reporting)

On July 10, 2026, the U.S. Department of War (DoW, the renamed Department of Defense) published the **fourth** tranche of declassified Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files on the public portal **https://www.war.gov/UFO/** under the **Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE)** program directed by President Donald J. Trump. It is the largest tranche to date to contain moving imagery held by the Pentagon's **All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)**.

The July 10 release adds **40 records** to the portal — **14 documents, 19 videos, four audio files and three images** — sourced from the Department of War, NASA, the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), and the Department of Energy. Per DoW, the previously classified holdings are drawn from investigations spanning 1947–2025 and are described as "unresolved." The Department framed the drop as a continuation of a **rolling declassification** effort, with the second and third tranches previously posted on May 22 and June 12, 2026.

### Key Contents Highlighted by DoW and Independent Reporting

- **AARO Cockpit and Sensor Video (2019 — Eastern United States).** File **DOW-UAP-PR112 "Unresolved UAP Report, Eastern United States, 2019"** contains an aviator's account of a **fast-moving rectangular object** whose flight behavior "could not be readily explained." The observer, described as a U.S. Air Force and U.S. Navy pilot with 28 years of service, reportedly wrote that the object exhibited **"flight characteristics unlike anything [the observer] had seen"** in that career.
- **Range-Fouler Encounter (2019).** A range-fouler report describes a small **rectangular object** caught on a surveillance-aircraft camera; military witnesses said it moved **faster than the aircraft's tracking system** could follow.
- **Atlantic Ocean Encounter (2020).** File **DOW-UAP-PR116 "Unresolved UAP Report, Atlantic Ocean, 2020"** describes a **"darker, maroonish"** object roughly 12–15 feet in height that "traveled with the wind," did not maneuver, and resembled a "large, somewhat deformed balloon."
- **Yellow Sea / East China Sea Sensor Video (2025).** Grainy multi-sensor / infrared imagery of unresolved UAP sightings from the western Pacific in 2025.
- **Gulf of America Encounter (2019).** Multi-sensor footage of an unresolved UAP incident over the Gulf of America (formerly Gulf of Mexico) in 2019.
- **Western United States (1996).** Legacy imagery of an unresolved sighting.
- **Pantex Plant Incident Report (September 2015).** A Department of Energy report describing an unidentified **diamond-shaped object** that entered restricted airspace over the **Pantex nuclear-weapons assembly and disassembly facility** near Amarillo, Texas.
- **Northeastern Orb Sighting (July 2025).** A video showing **two bright orbs** moving through the sky in tandem; witnesses described the motion as "silent and smooth."
- **Project Sign & Project Blue Book records.** Additional early-era U.S. Air Force UAP investigation records from the late-1940s / mid-1950s programs.
- **1955 CIA sighting analyses.**
- **1949 Los Alamos aerial-phenomena conference proceedings** (declassified copy).
- **NASA STS-80 imagery** (from the 1996 Space Shuttle Columbia mission).
- **FBI correspondence** relating to legacy UFO reports.

### Program Context

The July 10 release is the fourth batch under **PURSUE**, an interagency declassification program the President directed the Department of War to lead in February 2026 to "find, review, identify, declassify and publicly release unresolved UAP-related records and historical documents in the federal government's possession." Officials repeated that additional tranches will continue to be posted **on a rolling basis** to https://www.war.gov/UFO/ over the coming weeks and months.

Many of the July 10 videos were first publicly referenced in a **March 31, 2026** letter from Representative **Anna Paulina Luna (R-Fla.)** to U.S. Secretary of War **Pete Hegseth**, in which Luna requested public release of 46 videos tied to unresolved UAP incidents held by AARO.

### Reception

Coverage in mainstream outlets (CBS, NBC, ABC, Fortune, Interesting Engineering) and UAP-focused outlets (The Debrief, Liberation Times) characterized the fourth release as the **most video-heavy to date** but concluded, as with prior tranches, that the material contains **no conclusive evidence of extraterrestrial life** and that most of the videos show small, blurry objects captured with multi-sensor electro-optical targeting systems.

Fortune summarized prior tranches as containing "fish-scaled potatoes and red orbs" but "still no aliens." The Debrief noted that even with the added AARO cockpit footage, **"clarity remains elusive"**: individual encounter narratives are compelling but the raw sensor material is largely inconclusive without accompanying metadata.

Under the Trump administration's disclosure push, polling cited at the DC UAP Disclosure Forum earlier this year reported that nearly 90% of Republican and Democratic respondents said they want the government to release more UAP information.

## 简体中文译稿

### 标题

**美国战争部在 WAR.GOV/UFO 发布第四批"不明异常现象"（UAP）解密档案**

来源：美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）——2026 年 7 月 10 日新闻稿。

### 摘要（多源交叉核对合成）

2026 年 7 月 10 日，美国战争部（U.S. Department of War, DoW，即改组后的原国防部 Department of Defense）在公共门户 **https://www.war.gov/UFO/** 上，根据**总统不明异常现象遭遇启封与报告系统（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE）**计划，发布**第四批**解密的"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案。这是迄今为止在门户上发布的、包含**全域异常解析办公室（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）**所持动态影像最多的一批。

7 月 10 日的这一批**共 40 件——文档 14 件、视频 19 件、音频 4 件、图像 3 件**，来自战争部、美国国家航空航天局（NASA）、中央情报局（CIA）、联邦调查局（FBI）与能源部（Department of Energy）。战争部方面表示，本次公开的原属机密档案时间跨度为 1947 年至 2025 年，均归类为"未结案（unresolved）"。战争部将本次发布定性为**滚动式（on a rolling basis）**解密工作的延续；此前的第二批与第三批分别于 2026 年 5 月 22 日和 6 月 12 日上传。

### 战争部与外界报道共同强调的重点档案

- **AARO 座舱与传感器视频（2019 · 美国东部）**：档案 **DOW-UAP-PR112《未结案 UAP 报告 · 美国东部 · 2019》**记录一名飞行员对**一个高速运动的矩形物体**的目击，其飞行行为"无法轻易解释"。据称该目击者为美国空军（U.S. Air Force）与美国海军（U.S. Navy）飞行员，服役 28 年，形容该物体的**"飞行特征是我此前所见前所未见"**。
- **靶场入侵者遭遇（2019）**：一份"range-fouler"报告描述一架侦察机镜头捕捉到的**小型矩形物体**；军方目击者称其运动**速度超过飞机跟踪系统**的处理能力。
- **大西洋遭遇（2020）**：档案 **DOW-UAP-PR116《未结案 UAP 报告 · 大西洋 · 2020》**描述一个**颜色偏暗、近栗色（maroonish）**的物体，高约 12—15 英尺，"随风漂移"，未见机动，外观类似"一个变形的大型气球"。
- **黄海 / 东海传感器视频（2025）**：2025 年西太平洋方向未解 UAP 目击的多传感器 / 红外颗粒状影像。
- **美洲湾遭遇（2019）**：一段发生在美洲湾（Gulf of America，前墨西哥湾 Gulf of Mexico）2019 年一起未解 UAP 事件的多传感器影像。
- **美国西部（1996）**：一起历史目击的旧影像。
- **Pantex 工厂事件报告（2015 年 9 月）**：能源部一份报告，描述一枚不明的**菱形（diamond-shaped）**物体，进入了得州阿马里洛（Amarillo, Texas）附近的**潘特克斯核武器组装 / 拆解设施（Pantex Plant）**上空的禁飞空域。
- **美国东北部球体目击（2025 年 7 月）**：一段视频显示**两个明亮球体**在空中并列移动，目击者描述其运动"无声且平滑"。
- **"标志计划"（Project Sign）与"蓝皮书计划"（Project Blue Book）档案**：来自 1940 年代末至 1950 年代中期美国空军的早期 UAP 调查项目的补充档案。
- **1955 年 CIA 目击分析报告**。
- **1949 年洛斯阿拉莫斯（Los Alamos）空中现象研讨会会议纪要**（解密版）。
- **NASA STS-80 影像**（1996 年"哥伦比亚号"航天飞机任务）。
- **FBI 与历史 UFO 报告有关的通信**。

### 计划背景

7 月 10 日发布是 **PURSUE** 计划下的第四批。总统于 2026 年 2 月指示战争部牵头这一跨机构解密项目，任务是"快速查找、审阅、识别、解密并公开发布联邦政府持有的未解 UAP 相关档案与历史文件"。官员再次表示，未来数周与数月内将持续在 https://www.war.gov/UFO/ **滚动式**投放后续批次。

本次公开的许多视频，最早出现于 2026 年 3 月 31 日众议员 **安娜·保利娜·卢娜（Anna Paulina Luna，R-佛罗里达州）**致战争部部长 **皮特·海格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）** 的信件——她在信中要求公开 AARO 持有的 46 段与未解 UAP 事件相关的视频。

### 各方反应

主流媒体（CBS、NBC、ABC、Fortune、Interesting Engineering）与 UAP 垂直媒体（The Debrief、Liberation Times）的报道大体一致地指出，第四批是**迄今视频占比最高**的一次，但与此前批次相同，**未包含任何可确认外星生命存在的决定性证据**，绝大多数视频呈现为多传感器电光瞄准（EO/IR）系统拍到的模糊小物体。

《Fortune》曾以"鱼鳞土豆和红球体，但仍然没有外星人"总结此前批次；The Debrief 则指出，即便加入了 AARO 座舱影像，本次公开**"清晰度依旧难以捉摸"**——单个遭遇叙事本身颇具冲击力，但在缺乏配套元数据（metadata）的情况下，原始传感器素材大多无法给出结论。

在特朗普政府推动的公开叙事下，早前 DC "UAP 披露论坛（UAP Disclosure Forum）"援引的民调显示，共和党与民主党受访者中，均有近 90% 支持政府公开更多 UAP 信息。
