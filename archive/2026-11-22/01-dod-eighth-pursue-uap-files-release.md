### Official statement (verbatim excerpts)

> "Today, the Department of War is publishing the eighth release of
> declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files
> as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP
> Encounters (PURSUE). The eighth release is available now on WAR.GOV/UFO."
> — Sean Parnell, Chief Pentagon Spokesman

> "The Department of War and our agency partners are actively working on
> the next release of UAP files."

### Summary of the release (paraphrased)

The U.S. Department of War today posted the eighth batch of declassified
and historical UAP records on its WAR.GOV/UFO portal. The tranche comprises
43 records — 40 documents and three military videos — contributed by the
Department of War and the Central Intelligence Agency. With Friday's post,
the total number of records published under PURSUE reaches 521 since the
program's launch on May 8, 2026.

Highlighted items in this tranche include:

- The first full release of the 1953 report authored by Lockheed's
  Clarence L. "Kelly" Johnson, long cited in UFO history but previously
  available only in excerpted form.
- A 1952 Central Intelligence Agency evaluation summarizing hundreds of
  contemporaneous "flying saucer" sighting reports.
- The first Pentagon radar-track analysis of a UAP target, describing an
  object clocked at approximately Mach 2.5.
- A government contractor report that details plans for an underground
  vault intended to store "exotic material or tissue." A copy of this
  report surfaced on the open web a year ago, but Friday's release marks
  the first official Pentagon publication of it.
- Three military infrared and full-motion video files, including one
  labeled "PR168" showing seven airborne objects with periodic brightness
  variations.

The Department states that redactions in the materials are limited to
information needed "to protect the identity of eyewitnesses, the location
of government facilities, or potentially sensitive information about
military sites not related to UAP." The Pentagon reiterated that additional
files will be released on a rolling basis, and that the WAR.GOV/UFO
collection will continue to grow as more materials are declassified.

### Early outside analysis

UAP Science Advisory Council member Jacob Haqq-Misra published a
preliminary, not-yet-peer-reviewed assessment of one of the new videos
(PR168), suggesting that the seven objects' periodic infrared brightness
variation is consistent with birds in flapping flight rather than
anomalous propulsion. Harvard astronomer Avi Loeb, surveying the full
batch in a Medium essay, wrote that none of the newly released records
contain a measurement of technology that requires a non-human origin,
while flagging a document describing research into "poltergeist phenomena,
discarnate voices, [and] unusual creatures" at a Utah ranch as among the
more unusual items in the drop.

### 本次发布要点（转述）

美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）当天在其 WAR.GOV/UFO 门户发布
第八批已解密的历史性 UAP 档案。本批次共 43 份记录——40 份文件与 3
份军方视频——由战争部与中央情报局（Central Intelligence Agency，CIA）
共同贡献。随本周五的发布，PURSUE 计划自 2026 年 5 月 8 日启动以来，
累计公开的记录数量达到 521 份。

本批次中引起关注的材料包括：

- 1953 年由洛克希德（Lockheed）传奇工程师 Clarence L. "Kelly"
  Johnson 撰写报告的首次完整公开。该报告在 UFO 研究史上长期被引用，
  但此前仅有节选版流传。
- 1952 年中央情报局（CIA）对同期数百起"飞碟（flying saucer）"目击
  报告所作的评估。
- 五角大楼首份面向 UAP 目标的雷达航迹分析报告，描述一架航速约达
  2.5 马赫（Mach 2.5）的物体。
- 一份政府承包商报告，详述了用于存放"外来材料或组织（exotic
  material or tissue）"的地下金库建设方案。该报告的副本去年已在公开
  网络流出，但周五的发布是五角大楼官方首次公布该文件。
- 三份军方红外与 FMV（full-motion video，全动态视频）文件，其中一
  份编号 "PR168"，呈现七个空中目标存在周期性亮度变化。

战争部表示，材料中的涂黑处仅限于"为保护目击者身份、政府设施位置，
或与 UAP 无关的军事场所的潜在敏感信息"所必需的部分。五角大楼重申，
后续文件将滚动发布（rolling basis），WAR.GOV/UFO 档案库将随更多材料
的解密而持续扩充。

### 外部早期分析

UAP 科学咨询委员会（UAP Science Advisory Council）成员 Jacob
Haqq-Misra 对本批次视频 PR168 发布了未经同行评审的初步评估，认为七
个目标的红外亮度周期性变化更符合鸟类扑翼飞行的特征，而非异常推进。
哈佛天文学家 Avi Loeb 在 Medium 刊文综观本批 43 份材料时写道，新公
开的记录中没有任何一项包含要求非人类起源的技术测量结果；同时他特别
点名其中一份关于犹他州（Utah）某牧场所谓"幽灵现象（poltergeist
phenomena）、无实体之声（discarnate voices）、异常生物（unusual
creatures）"研究的文件，称其为本次发布中较为离奇的条目之一。
