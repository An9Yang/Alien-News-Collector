**Department of War Publishes Second Release of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Files on WAR.GOV/UFO**
*Immediate Release · Friday, May 22, 2026*

The Department of War is publishing the second release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE), available at [WAR.GOV/UFO](https://www.war.gov/ufo/).

At the direction of President Donald J. Trump, the Department of War is overseeing a multiagency effort to expeditiously find, review, identify, declassify and publicly release unresolved Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena-related records and historical documents in the federal government's possession.

[Summary, reconstructed from coverage] Friday's tranche comprises **64 files: six PDF documents, seven audio files, and 51 video files** drawn from military branches, federal agencies, and civilian reports. Many of the videos capture encounters in U.S. Central Command's area of responsibility between 2018 and 2023, including over the Persian Gulf. The release additionally includes radar imagery and first-hand witness statements from intelligence officers and pilots.

### Statement from Secretary of War Pete Hegseth

> "The Department of War is in lockstep with President Trump to bring unprecedented transparency regarding our government's understanding of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena. These files, hidden behind classifications, have long fueled justified speculation — and it's time the American people see it for themselves."
> — *Pete Hegseth, Secretary of War*

### Site traffic and forward plan

Since the site's launch on **May 8, 2026**, **WAR.GOV/UFO has received over 1 billion hits worldwide**, highlighting the unprecedented levels of interest in both this topic and the Trump administration's historic transparency effort. The collection continues to be housed on WAR.GOV/UFO, and additional files will be released on a rolling basis. The Department of War and its agency partners are actively working on the third release of UAP files, which will be announced in the near future.

### Notable items within Release 02 (per press coverage and the PURSUE document index)

- **Lake Huron infrared video** — A 46-second black-and-white infrared clip titled along the lines of *"USAF ANG F-16C (callsign [redacted]) Shoots Down UAP over Lake Huron with [Weapon System], 12 Feb 2023."* The footage was captured by an F-16C of the Minnesota Air National Guard's 148th Fighter Wing (Duluth) intercepting an octagonal object at approximately 20,000 feet in Canadian airspace above Lake Huron. The clip shows a distinct area of thermal contrast being tracked at roughly the 11-second mark; at the ~20-second mark the footage depicts what AARO describes as a "kinetic interaction" between two contrast areas — the AIM-9X Sidewinder missile impacting the target at approximately 2:42 p.m. local. An initial Sidewinder reportedly missed; a second struck and destroyed the object. AARO's working assessment is that the object was most likely balloon-like in nature.
- **Senior U.S. intelligence officer narrative (ODNI-UAP-D001)** — A first-person account by a currently serving "senior intelligence officer" who, with a colleague and two pilots, departed a Joint Operations Center aboard a military helicopter to investigate prior UAP sightings in late 2025, and recounts "a series of close UAP encounters lasting over an hour."
- **CIA 1973 Intelligence Information Report (CIA-UAP-D001)** — a declassified Cold-War-era intelligence information report concerning a USSR-context UAP observation. (PDF indexed under `medialink/ufo/052226/release_02/documents/CIA-UAP-D001_Intelligence_Information_Report_USSR_1973.pdf`.)
- Multiple cockpit / sensor videos referenced as featuring "spherical objects" and "orange orbs," shapes military personnel and AARO officials have repeatedly flagged in recent years.

**美国战争部发布第二批不明异常现象（UAP）档案，登录 WAR.GOV/UFO**
*立即发布 · 2026 年 5 月 22 日（星期五）*

美国战争部（Department of War）正在发布第二批解密的历史性不明异常现象（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案，作为"总统启封与 UAP 遭遇报告系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，简称 PURSUE）的一部分，公众可在 [WAR.GOV/UFO](https://www.war.gov/ufo/) 查阅。

按照唐纳德·J·特朗普（Donald J. Trump）总统的指令，战争部正牵头一项跨部门工作，加速查找、审阅、识别、解密并公开联邦政府所掌握的尚未解决的 UAP 相关档案与历史文件。

[概述，基于媒体报道重建] 周五公布的这一批共 **64 份文件，包括 6 份 PDF 文档、7 份音频文件与 51 份视频文件**，材料来源涵盖各军种、联邦机构以及民间报告。其中相当数量的视频记录了 2018 年至 2023 年间发生在美国中央司令部（U.S. Central Command）辖区内（含波斯湾上空）的事件。本批次还包括雷达图像，以及来自情报军官和飞行员的第一人称证词。

### 战争部长 Pete Hegseth 声明

> "战争部与特朗普总统步调一致，致力于在政府对不明异常现象的理解问题上推动前所未有的透明度。这些长期被密级所掩盖的档案，曾合理地激发了公众的揣测——是时候让美国人民亲眼看看了。"
> ——*Pete Hegseth（皮特·赫格塞斯），战争部长*

### 网站流量与后续安排

自 **2026 年 5 月 8 日**网站上线以来，**WAR.GOV/UFO 全球访问量已超过 10 亿次**，凸显公众对该议题以及特朗普政府这一具有历史意义的透明化举措的空前关注。资料集将继续托管于 WAR.GOV/UFO，后续档案将以滚动方式发布。战争部及其合作机构正在积极筹备第三批 UAP 档案，将在不久之后宣布发布安排。

### Release 02 中的几项值得关注的内容（基于媒体报道与 PURSUE 文档索引）

- **休伦湖（Lake Huron）红外视频** —— 一段时长 46 秒的黑白红外影像，标题大致为 *"USAF ANG F-16C（呼号 [已隐去]）以 [武器系统] 在休伦湖上空击落 UAP，2023 年 2 月 12 日"*。影像来自明尼苏达州空军国民警卫队第 148 战斗机联队（驻德卢斯/Duluth）的一架 F-16C，于加拿大领空、休伦湖上空约 20,000 英尺高度拦截一个外形呈八角形的物体。视频在约第 11 秒可见一处明显的热成像对比区域被持续跟踪；约第 20 秒处出现 AARO 所称的"动能交互"——AIM-9X 响尾蛇导弹击中目标，本地时间约 14:42。报道称首发响尾蛇未中，第二发命中并摧毁目标。AARO 当前的工作判断是：该物体最可能为气球类目标。
- **美方现役高级情报官的亲历证词（编号 ODNI-UAP-D001）** —— 一位现役"高级情报官"（senior intelligence officer）以第一人称叙述：他与一名同事及两名飞行员于 2025 年下半年自一处联合作战中心（Joint Operations Center）起飞，乘军用直升机前往复查此前的 UAP 目击点，并称机组与他本人经历了"一连串持续超过一小时的近距离 UAP 遭遇"。
- **中央情报局（CIA）1973 年情报信息报告（CIA-UAP-D001）** —— 一份冷战时期的解密情报信息报告，涉及一起以苏联（USSR）为背景的 UAP 目击事件。该 PDF 索引路径为 `medialink/ufo/052226/release_02/documents/CIA-UAP-D001_Intelligence_Information_Report_USSR_1973.pdf`。
- 多段座舱/传感器视频被报道含有"球形物体"与"橙色光球"（orange orbs），这正是近年军方人员与 AARO 反复提及的形状特征。
