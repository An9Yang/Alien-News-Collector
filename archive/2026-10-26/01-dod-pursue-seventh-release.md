## Original (English)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — October 23, 2026**
**U.S. Department of War, Office of Public Affairs**

The Department of War today published the seventh release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files on WAR.GOV/UFO as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). The seventh tranche adds 28 new records to the public archive, bringing the running total to 478 files across seven releases since the program's inaugural tranche on May 8, 2026.

Unlike previous releases, which included a mixture of videos, imagery, and documents, this tranche consists entirely of written material. All 28 files in Release 07 are PDF documents; no videos, audio, or stand-alone imagery are included.

The headline document is an All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) memorandum designated **DOW-UAP-D176**, which describes a June 2026 encounter near a sensitive national-security facility in the western United States. According to the memorandum, federal special agents reported observing a matte-black, triangular object operating in what one summary describes as "absolute acoustic silence." The reporting individuals describe a triangular object that rose vertically, remained stationary, then banked and descended into a canyon. Independent coverage of the memorandum reports approximate dimensions of nine to fifteen feet across, six feet deep, and two feet thick. Witnesses stated that the craft exhibited none of the usual aircraft lighting, produced no audible sound, and could not be detected by available thermal equipment. One witness account places the object hovering approximately 200 to 300 feet above an elevated ridgeline for about twenty seconds before accelerating across a deep canyon in two to three seconds. Investigators have not identified the object, have not verified its reported performance, and have found no physical evidence on the ground.

The release also includes:
- A declassified report on alleged paranormal activity at **Skinwalker Ranch**, Utah.
- Historical sighting reports from residents of **Catalina Island**, California, covering the period 1949 to 1955.
- Documents detailing coordination between **NASA** and the Department of Defense on the handling of suspected fragments of space vehicles.
- Additional historical UAP sighting records from the 1940s and 1950s.

The release page notes that redactions have been applied to protect the identity of eyewitnesses, the location of government facilities, and potentially sensitive information about military sites unrelated to UAP activity. The Department emphasized that the records offer no conclusions and no theories about the nature of the reported objects, and that none of the files released to date through PURSUE establish the existence of extraterrestrial technology or non-human intelligence.

"The Department of War and our agency partners are actively working on the next release of UAP files," the department stated. The Department reiterated that files will continue to be released on a rolling basis as they are discovered, reviewed, and declassified. The complete archive is housed at WAR.GOV/UFO.

PURSUE was established by presidential direction earlier in 2026 to identify, review, declassify, and publicly release UAP-related records held by U.S. federal agencies. The initiative is overseen by the Department of War with support from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), the Department of Energy, NASA, and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). The first PURSUE tranche was released on May 8, 2026, with subsequent releases on May 22, June 12, July 10, August 7, September 18, and October 23, 2026.

## 简体中文译稿

**【即时发布 · 2026 年 10 月 23 日】**
**美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）公共事务办公室**

战争部（Department of War）今日在 WAR.GOV/UFO 发布了第七批解密及历史"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案。本次发布属于总统 UAP 遭遇解封与报告系统（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE）项目的一部分。第七批共新增 28 份记录，使自 2026 年 5 月 8 日该项目首批档案公开以来，公开档案累计数达 478 份。

与此前几批（混合视频、图像与文件）不同，本批次全部为书面材料。第七批（Release 07）全部 28 份文件均为 PDF 文档，不包含视频、音频或独立图像资料。

本次最受关注的核心文件为全领域异常解决办公室（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）编号 **DOW-UAP-D176** 的备忘录，内容记述了 2026 年 6 月在美国西部某敏感国家安全设施附近发生的一次目击事件。据该备忘录描述，多名联邦特工（federal special agents）报告观察到一枚哑光黑色（matte-black）三角形物体，该物体处于一份独立报道所称的"绝对声学静默"（absolute acoustic silence）状态下运行。报告人员描述该三角形物体先垂直上升、保持静止，随后侧倾并俯冲进入一处峡谷。关于该备忘录的独立报道给出该物体的大致尺寸为宽 9 至 15 英尺（约 2.7 至 4.6 米）、深 6 英尺（约 1.8 米）、厚 2 英尺（约 0.6 米）。目击者表示，该飞行器未显示任何常规飞行器通常具有的灯光，未发出可听见的声音，且现场可用的热成像设备（thermal equipment）亦无法探测到热信号。某份目击陈述称，该物体在一道海拔较高的山脊线（elevated ridgeline）上方约 200 至 300 英尺（约 60 至 90 米）处悬停约 20 秒，随后在两至三秒内加速穿越一道深峡谷。调查人员迄今未确认该物体身份，未核实其所称性能，亦未在地面发现任何实物证据。

此次发布还包括以下材料：
- 关于犹他州"**穿行者牧场**"（Skinwalker Ranch, Utah）所谓超自然活动的一份解密报告。
- 1949 年至 1955 年间加利福尼亚州**卡塔琳娜岛**（Catalina Island）居民的历史目击报告。
- 详述**美国国家航空航天局**（NASA）与国防部（DoD）就疑似航天器残骸处理所进行协调工作的档案。
- 1940 年代至 1950 年代的其他历史 UAP 目击记录。

发布页面注明，为保护目击者身份、政府设施位置以及与 UAP 无关的敏感军事设施信息，相关档案已进行必要的删节处理。战争部强调，此次公开的记录对所报告物体的性质未作任何结论，亦未提出任何理论；迄今通过 PURSUE 发布的所有档案均未证实外星技术（extraterrestrial technology）或非人类智能（non-human intelligence）的存在。

战争部在声明中表示："战争部与各合作机构正在积极准备下一批 UAP 文件的发布工作。"该部重申，将在文件被发现、审查并解密后，以滚动方式持续公开。全部档案存放于 WAR.GOV/UFO。

PURSUE 项目由总统于 2026 年早些时候下令设立，目的是识别、审查、解密并公开发布由美国联邦各机构保存的 UAP 相关记录。该倡议由战争部主导，国家情报总监办公室（Office of the Director of National Intelligence, ODNI）、能源部（Department of Energy）、NASA 以及联邦调查局（Federal Bureau of Investigation, FBI）协同支持。首批 PURSUE 档案于 2026 年 5 月 8 日公开，其后各批次分别发布于 5 月 22 日、6 月 12 日、7 月 10 日、8 月 7 日、9 月 18 日和 10 月 23 日。
