## Original (English)

**WASHINGTON — Sept. 18, 2026.** The U.S. Department of War announced today the sixth release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files, published as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). The newly disclosed material is available at WAR.GOV/UFO, and additional files will continue to be added to the site on a rolling basis.

At the direction of President Donald J. Trump, the Department of War is overseeing a multiagency effort to expeditiously find, review, identify, declassify and publicly release unresolved UAP-related records and historical documents in the federal government's possession. PURSUE began releasing records in May 2026, with previous batches published on May 8, May 22, June 12, July 10 and Aug. 7. Records have come from multiple federal agencies, including the Department of War, FBI, CIA, State Department and the Executive Office of the President.

Friday's release contains a total of 71 files — 67 provided by the Department of War and four from local law enforcement in Colorado. The package comprises 55 PDF files, 15 video files and one audio file. Sixty-four of the 71 files remain partially redacted. Document dates range from 1952 to 2025.

The most substantive documents in the sixth batch relate to a formerly secretive military initiative known as the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), which the Defense Intelligence Agency established in 2008 to study various aerospace threats decades into the future. The release includes 37 "Defense Intelligence Reference Documents," or DIRDs, produced under AAWSAP and covering topics such as warp drives, wormholes, antigravity propulsion, negative-mass propulsion and invisibility cloaking. All 37 DIRDs have been publicly available for years — including via Freedom of Information Act releases by the DIA — but Friday's versions include details the agency had previously withheld, such as the names of the offices that produced the documents.

Also among the historical material is digitized footage filmed near Tremonton, Utah, in July 1952 by U.S. Navy Chief Warrant Officer Delbert C. Newhouse, who reported observing a group of bright airborne objects. Several other files date from the 1950s and are tied to the U.S. Air Force's Project Blue Book, the service's long-running effort to investigate UFO sightings. Among them is a 1952 document concerning an unidentified object rising from the sea off Okinawa in November 1950.

The Department of War said the sixth release also contains material describing physiological effects and unexplained injuries that AAWSAP researchers linked to alleged encounters with unknown craft, along with additional imagery, correspondence and analytical notes from Cold War–era investigations.

PURSUE is not itself a UAP investigative body; that mission continues to sit with the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO). Rather, PURSUE is an interagency effort to identify, review, declassify and publicly release UAP-related records held by U.S. federal agencies. Officials said further releases will follow, and that the Department of War is coordinating with the White House and other participating agencies on the pace and scope of ongoing disclosures.

Members of the public and researchers can access the full set of released files at https://www.war.gov/UFO/.

## 简体中文译稿

**华盛顿 — 2026 年 9 月 18 日**。美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）今日宣布，作为"UAP 遭遇事件总统解封与报告系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，简称 PURSUE）的组成部分，正式公开第六批已解密及历史存档的不明异常现象（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena，UAP）档案。新增材料可在 WAR.GOV/UFO 页面获取，未来还将继续以滚动方式追加。

在总统唐纳德·J·特朗普（Donald J. Trump）指示下，战争部正牵头协调一项多部门行动，尽快查找、审阅、鉴别、解密并向社会公开联邦政府手中尚未结案的 UAP 相关档案与历史文件。PURSUE 于 2026 年 5 月启动首批发布，此前的五次公开时间分别为 5 月 8 日、5 月 22 日、6 月 12 日、7 月 10 日与 8 月 7 日。参与提供档案的机构包括战争部、联邦调查局（FBI）、中央情报局（CIA）、国务院（State Department）及总统行政办公室（Executive Office of the President）。

本周五（9 月 18 日）公开的一批共计 71 份文件，其中 67 份来自战争部，另 4 份由科罗拉多州（Colorado）地方执法机构提供。全部资料包含 55 份 PDF 文件、15 段视频与 1 段音频；71 份文件中有 64 份仍存在不同程度的涂黑处理（redaction）。文件成文时间跨度为 1952 年至 2025 年。

第六批中份量最重的一组文件，涉及一项此前较为隐秘的军方项目——"高级航空航天武器系统应用计划"（Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program，AAWSAP）。该项目由国防情报局（Defense Intelligence Agency，DIA）于 2008 年设立，用以研究未来几十年内可能出现的多种航空航天威胁。此次公开中，包含 37 份在 AAWSAP 框架下产出的"国防情报参考文件"（Defense Intelligence Reference Documents，DIRDs），议题涵盖曲速引擎（warp drive）、虫洞（wormhole）、反重力（antigravity）、负质量推进（negative-mass propulsion）以及隐形披风技术（invisibility cloaking）等。这 37 份 DIRDs 早在多年前即已通过《信息自由法》（Freedom of Information Act，FOIA）等渠道对外公布，但本次版本恢复了此前被隐去的部分细节，例如出具文件的具体处室名称。

历史档案部分还包括 1952 年 7 月由美国海军准尉德尔伯特·C·纽豪斯（Chief Warrant Officer Delbert C. Newhouse）在犹他州（Utah）特雷蒙顿（Tremonton）附近拍摄、如今经过数字化处理的一段影像，纽豪斯当时报告称观察到一组明亮的空中不明物体。另有多份 1950 年代的文件与美国空军"蓝皮书计划"（Project Blue Book）——该军种长期开展的 UFO 目击调查行动——直接相关；其中一份 1952 年的文件，讨论 1950 年 11 月在冲绳（Okinawa）近海升空的一处不明物体。

战争部方面表示，第六批公开材料还包含 AAWSAP 研究人员将某些生理反应及原因不明的伤害与"疑似不明飞行器接触事件"相互关联的记录，以及冷战时期调查产生的更多影像、通讯往来与分析笔记。

PURSUE 本身并非负责调查 UAP 事件的机构——该职责仍归属"全域异常识别办公室"（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office，AARO）；PURSUE 定位为一项跨部门行动，任务是识别、审阅、解密并公开美国联邦各部门手中的 UAP 相关记录。官方称，后续发布将持续进行；战争部正与白宫（White House）及其他参与部门协调此后公开的节奏与范围。

公众与研究人员可通过 https://www.war.gov/UFO/ 访问已公开的全部文件。
