## Original (English)

The United States government has established a new interagency body to coordinate UAP investigations, improve the collection and analysis of relevant data, and assist with the declassification of UAP-related information, according to an official from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI).

According to the ODNI official, to support the President's directive on UAP transparency, ODNI — alongside the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the Department of War (DOW) — established a UAP Governance Board to provide guidance, recommendations and coordination at the interagency level, bringing together military, law enforcement, the Intelligence Community, and other civilian agencies.

The stated mission of the Governance Board, as detailed in the board's charter, is to serve as an interagency body that can use each member's capabilities and unique authorities to cohesively address national security threats posed by UAP.

The charter directs the board to integrate and optimize interagency processes for the investigation of UAP incidents and for the collection and analysis procedures applied to UAP data, with the explicit objective of delivering more effective support to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO). Additionally, the board is required to assist in the timely coordination of declassification of UAP-related information in accordance with Executive Order 13526.

The UAP Governance Board held its first meeting on June 16, 2026, and convenes representatives from military, law enforcement, the Intelligence Community, and other civilian agencies.

The UAP Governance Board is supported by several advisory groups with expertise outside the U.S. government. Among them is the new UAP Science Advisory Council, which Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb announced he had been tasked by the White House, AARO, ODNI, the FBI, and the Intelligence Community to lead and assemble. Loeb, who also leads the Galileo Project, has named five founding members of the council drawn from data science, instrumentation, astrophysics, and human-factors research backgrounds.

## 简体中文译稿

据美国国家情报总监办公室（Office of the Director of National Intelligence，ODNI）一名官员表示，美国政府已成立一个新的跨机构机构，用以统筹不明异常现象（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena，UAP）相关调查、改进相关数据的收集与分析，并协助 UAP 相关信息的解密工作。

该 ODNI 官员表示，为支持总统就 UAP 透明度问题作出的指示，ODNI 与联邦调查局（FBI）、战争部（Department of War，DOW，即原国防部，现行政府于 2026 年改用此名）共同设立了一个 "UAP 治理委员会"（UAP Governance Board），负责在跨机构层面提供指导、建议与协调，整合军方、执法、情报界以及其他文职机构的力量。

委员会章程明确，其设立宗旨是作为一个跨机构机构，整合并运用各成员单位的能力与法定授权，以协同方式应对 UAP 所带来的国家安全威胁。

章程要求委员会对 UAP 事件调查及 UAP 数据的采集与分析流程进行跨机构层面的整合与优化，明确目标是为全域异常现象应对办公室（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office，AARO）提供更有效的支持。此外，章程要求该委员会依据美国第 13526 号行政命令（Executive Order 13526），协助及时协调 UAP 相关信息的解密工作。

UAP 治理委员会已于 2026 年 6 月 16 日召开首次会议，与会代表分别来自军方、执法机构、情报界以及其他文职机构。

UAP 治理委员会的工作由若干外部专家咨询小组提供支撑，其中包括新成立的 "UAP 科学顾问理事会"（UAP Science Advisory Council）。哈佛大学天体物理学家阿维・洛布（Avi Loeb）此前宣布，他已受白宫、AARO、ODNI、FBI 及情报界其他成员单位委托，牵头组建该理事会。洛布同时担任 "伽利略项目"（Galileo Project）负责人，目前已宣布从数据科学、仪器开发、天体物理与人因心理学等领域提名五位创始成员。
