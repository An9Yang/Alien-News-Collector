## Original (English)

> **注：本文为美联社/CBS 新闻同源通稿与《华盛顿邮报》报道的关键事实摘要。原文位于《华盛顿邮报》付费墙之后，本次抓取无法取得逐段原文；以下信息汇总自公开可见的摘要、AP 通讯社/CBS 新闻镜像与同日多家美媒报道。完整原文需登录订阅。**

The Pentagon on Friday released a third batch of formerly classified files on Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), the latest tranche of records made public through the Department of War's "Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters" (PURSUE) initiative announced earlier this year by President Donald Trump.

The June 12 release comprises 72 items in total, including 53 documents and 10 images drawn from the CIA, the FBI, NASA, the Department of Defense and other unspecified federal agencies, along with six videos and three NASA audio recordings. The materials were posted to the Department of War's PURSUE landing page at war.gov/ufo.

Among the new files are descriptions of unusual objects whose observers struggled to categorize: a rotating disc reported to have emitted beams of light; a shining red orb "of a hue the observer had never seen before"; and an object compared variously to a potato and a bean, but covered in what witnesses described as a coat of shimmering, fish-like scales.

Several of the documents detail encounters reported by federal law enforcement officers in 2023, in which five agents reported seeing strange orbs on the horizon. The release also includes a July 2008 CIA report describing a UFO sighting above Harare International Airport in Zimbabwe, in which witnesses said the object was disc-like in shape with a hollow center and a series of rotating lights on the underside of the airframe.

A new report dated June 5, 2026, signed by Dr. Jon T. Kosloski, director of the Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), was bundled with the release. In it, Kosloski references an incident involving an orange "mother" orb that appeared to launch smaller red orbs, and notes that roughly 40 percent of phenomena reported to AARO continue to lack a reasonable prosaic explanation and remain unresolved.

The release also packages historical audio. Over two hours of recordings from the Apollo 16 mission debrief, recorded in 1972, were included; in one segment a speaker makes an off-handed comment about an "alien starbase" while discussing experimental data. A separate audio excerpt from a November 1962 interview captures CBS anchor Walter Cronkite asking NASA astronaut Gordon Cooper about his views on UFOs.

The 72 files released on Friday do not contain the kind of blockbuster revelation Trump has repeatedly teased in public remarks. There is no conclusive evidence of alien life and no acknowledgment of any government cover-up. But the files do disclose new details about a number of recent sightings and shed additional light on how the government has attempted to explain — and in many cases has been unable to explain — what witnesses have reported.

The Department of War said additional documents and videos will be released on a rolling basis on war.gov/ufo. Representative Anna Paulina Luna (R-Fla.), who in a March 31, 2026 letter to Secretary of War Pete Hegseth requested the declassification of 46 AARO-held UAP videos, has previously said a forthcoming installment of PURSUE is expected to include dozens of those videos, several reportedly depicting spherical or "transmedium" objects observed transitioning between air and water.

## 简体中文译稿

> **说明：原文位于《华盛顿邮报》付费墙之后，本次抓取仅能获取摘要内容，无法翻译全文。以下中文译稿对应上文同源事实摘要。完整原文需登录订阅。**

周五（6 月 12 日），五角大楼（Pentagon）公开了第三批此前列为机密的"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案。这批档案是本年度由唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump）总统宣布、由美国战争部（Department of War, DOW）牵头的"总统 UAP 事件解密与报告系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE）计划下，向公众公开的最新一批材料。

6 月 12 日的这次发布共含 72 件资料，其中包括 53 份文件、10 幅图像，来源涵盖中央情报局（CIA）、联邦调查局（FBI）、美国国家航空航天局（NASA）、国防部（Department of Defense）以及若干未具名的其他联邦机构；此外还有 6 段视频和 3 段 NASA 音频。所有材料均上传至战争部 PURSUE 专题页面 war.gov/ufo。

新材料中包括若干目击者难以归类的异常物体描述：一只据报会射出光束的旋转圆盘；一颗"目击者从未见过这种色调"的发光红色球体；以及一个被同时比作土豆与豆子、但表面覆盖"如鱼鳞般闪烁鳞片"的物体。

数份文件详细记述了 2023 年由联邦执法人员上报的目击事件，其中 5 名特工报告在地平线方向看到奇异的光球。这批资料还收录了 2008 年 7 月一份 CIA 报告，记录了在津巴布韦哈拉雷国际机场（Harare International Airport）上空目击的一次 UFO 事件：目击者称该物体呈碟形、中心中空，机体下方有一圈旋转的灯。

附在本次发布中的，还有一份签署于 2026 年 6 月 5 日的新报告，作者是五角大楼"全域异常处置办公室"（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）主任乔恩·T·科斯洛斯基博士（Dr. Jon T. Kosloski）。报告中提及一起涉及"橙色母体球"似乎释放出多枚较小红色球体的事件，并指出在 AARO 接报的现象中，约 40% 至今仍缺乏合理常规解释，归为未结案。

本次发布还附带了一批历史音频。其中包括 1972 年录制的"阿波罗 16 号"（Apollo 16）任务汇报，时长超过两小时；在一段录音里，发言者在讨论实验数据时随口提到了"外星人星基地"（alien starbase）。另一段 1962 年 11 月的采访音频，是 CBS 主持人沃尔特·克朗凯特（Walter Cronkite）就 UFO 问题询问 NASA 宇航员戈登·库珀（Gordon Cooper）的对话片段。

周五公开的这 72 份资料中，并未出现特朗普此前在公开场合反复暗示过的"重磅揭露"——既没有外星生命存在的决定性证据，也没有任何官方对所谓"政府掩盖"的承认。但这些文件确实披露了若干近期目击事件的新细节，并进一步展示了美国政府如何尝试解释——以及在许多情况下未能解释——目击者所报告的现象。

战争部表示，后续将通过 war.gov/ufo 按滚动方式继续发布更多文件与视频。佛罗里达州联邦众议员安娜·保利娜·卢娜（Anna Paulina Luna, R-Fla.）此前曾于 2026 年 3 月 31 日致函战争部长皮特·赫格塞斯（Pete Hegseth），要求解密 AARO 持有的 46 段 UAP 视频。她此前透露，PURSUE 下一期预计将包含其中多段视频，其中数段据称显示球状物体或"跨介质"（transmedium）物体在空中与水下之间穿梭。
