**Byline & dateline.** Christopher Sharp, *Liberation Times*, 15 October 2026.

**Lede.** Former U.S. Army sniper Nick Betts recounted an alleged deadly encounter with reptilian-looking beings on the Liberty Risk podcast, released on 5 December 2025. According to the account relayed by Betts, an unnamed man claimed U.S. personnel entered an underground installation near Area 51, killed three beings, and lost one of their own.

Betts replied that a joke of that kind would not normally cost someone their job, noting that his source was later removed from his position.

**Operational details alleged.** Per the account: three reptilian-looking beings were killed inside the underground installation; one U.S. operator died during the engagement; the dead American's body was reportedly cremated against the deceased's religious wishes, and his death was recorded on paper as a training accident.

**Framing / wider context.** The article places the Betts account alongside claims by former intelligence officer David Grusch, noting that Betts's second-hand description was first aired nearly ten months before Grusch's appearance on *The Joe Rogan Experience* on 30 September 2026, in which Grusch described similar reptilian-looking entities. Liberation Times notes that no independent corroboration of the Betts account appears to exist, and treats the piece as a report *about* an allegation rather than a confirmation of one.
