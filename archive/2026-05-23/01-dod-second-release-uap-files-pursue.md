## Original (English)

**WASHINGTON — May 22, 2026.** The Department of War today published the second release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE).

At the direction of President Donald J. Trump, the Department of War is overseeing a multi-agency effort to expeditiously find, review, identify, declassify and publicly release unresolved Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena-related records and historical documents in the federal government's possession.

"The Department of War is in lockstep with President Trump to bring unprecedented transparency regarding our government's understanding of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)," Secretary of War Pete Hegseth said in a statement accompanying the release. "These files, hidden behind classifications, have long fueled justified speculation. It's time the American people see it for themselves."

The newly published materials have been posted on WAR.GOV/UFO, continuing a rolling disclosure effort that began on May 8, 2026 with the first PURSUE tranche. According to the Department, WAR.GOV/UFO has received more than one billion hits worldwide since its launch two weeks ago.

Friday's release adds more than fifty additional video files to the public archive, along with several PDF documents and audio recordings. Officials said the package includes more than forty videos specifically requested by lawmakers in recent weeks, among them footage from a February 12, 2023 incident in which a U.S. Air Force F-16C — operating in the U.S. Northern Command area of responsibility — engaged an unidentified object over Lake Huron with an AIM-9X Sidewinder missile. Other items in the second tranche include first-hand witness accounts from military personnel and intelligence officers, a 2025 statement from a senior intelligence officer describing being "virtually speechless" after observing two large orbs alongside their helicopter, audio excerpts from NASA missions, and additional declassified material from the Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO).

Under President Trump's leadership, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence is coordinating the Intelligence Community's declassification effort with the Department of War to ensure a careful, comprehensive review of UAP-related holdings and to provide the American people with maximum transparency.

The Department reiterated that none of the material released to date provides confirmation of extraterrestrial origin or advanced non-human technology. AARO has stated that the majority of cases remain unresolved due to insufficient sensor data or incomplete observational records. A third PURSUE release is already in preparation and is expected to be announced "in the near future."

## 简体中文译稿

**华盛顿，2026 年 5 月 22 日电。** 美国战争部（Department of War，下称"战争部"）今日发布第二批已解密及历史"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案，作为"总统 UAP 接触解封与上报系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，简称 PURSUE）计划的延续。

在唐纳德·特朗普（Donald J. Trump）总统的指示下，战争部正牵头一项跨机构的工作，迅速查找、审阅、识别、解密并公开发布美国联邦政府所掌握的、尚未结案的 UAP 相关档案与历史文件。

"战争部与特朗普总统步调一致，致力于以前所未有的透明度公开政府对'不明异常现象'（UAP）的认知，"战争部长皮特·赫格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）在随发布稿一同发表的声明中表示，"这些被密级遮蔽的档案，长期以来助长了民众完全合理的猜测。是时候让美国民众亲眼看一看了。"

新公开的资料已发布于 WAR.GOV/UFO 网站，延续了 5 月 8 日 PURSUE 计划首批档案启动以来的滚动披露节奏。战争部表示，该网站自两周前上线以来，全球访问量已突破 10 亿次。

本周五（5 月 22 日）这一批向公开档案库新增了 50 多份视频文件，外加若干 PDF 文档和音频录音。官员表示，本次发布包含逾 40 段近几周内由国会议员专门点名要求公开的视频，其中包括一段拍摄于 2023 年 2 月 12 日的画面——当时一架隶属美国空军的 F-16C 战斗机（隶属于美国北方司令部辖区）使用 AIM-9X "响尾蛇"导弹击落了一架在密歇根州休伦湖（Lake Huron）上空出现的不明物体。第二批档案还包含来自现役军人和情报官员的第一手目击陈述，一份 2025 年某资深情报官员的书面陈述（描述其在执行任务时，目睹两枚巨大光球与其搭乘的直升机并行飞行后，自己"几乎说不出话"）、NASA 任务音频节选，以及五角大楼"全域异常解析办公室"（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）的更多解密资料。

在特朗普总统的领导下，美国国家情报总监办公室（Office of the Director of National Intelligence, ODNI）正在与战争部协调情报界的解密工作，以确保对 UAP 相关馆藏进行审慎、全面的审阅，向美国人民提供最大限度的透明度。

战争部重申，迄今为止公开的所有材料**均不能**证明这些现象源自地外文明或属于先进的非人类技术。AARO 此前已说明，多数案例由于传感器数据不足或观测记录不完整，至今仍无法定论。第三批 PURSUE 档案已在筹备中，预计将"在不久的将来"对外宣布。
