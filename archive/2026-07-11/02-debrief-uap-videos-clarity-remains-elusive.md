**Pentagon Releases New Batch of UAP Videos and Historical Files, But Clarity Remains Elusive**

The Debrief reports that on Friday, July 10, 2026, the U.S. Department of War (DoW)
published its fourth official batch of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files under
the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). The tranche
adds newly declassified imagery, historical documents, and previously unseen AARO video to
the growing WAR.GOV/UFO archive — but, in The Debrief's assessment, does little to resolve
the underlying mystery of what the objects actually are.

Per The Debrief's rundown, the batch consists of 40 items: 14 documents, 19 videos, 4 audio
files, and 3 images, drawn from the DoW, NASA, the CIA, the FBI, and the Department of
Energy. Most of the videos, the outlet notes, show small, blurry objects captured by
multi-sensor electro-optical targeting systems on board U.S. fighter aircraft, uncrewed
aerial vehicles, or civilian aircraft imaging systems. The Debrief observes that the imagery
resembles the kind of low-resolution footage that has characterized prior PURSUE tranches:
compelling enough to raise questions, but not high enough in fidelity to force conclusions.

The Debrief highlights several standout items:

- **1949 Los Alamos "green fireballs" conference transcript.** Manhattan Project–era
  scientists — including Edward Teller, Norris E. Bradbury, and meteor expert Lincoln
  LaPaz — debated a series of unexplained horizontally-moving bright-green objects observed
  over the New Mexico nuclear complex in late 1948. LaPaz is quoted in the transcript
  saying his own December 12, 1948 sighting was "most certainly not a conventional meteorite
  fall." The Debrief calls this one of the earliest formally documented, U.S.
  government–led UAP investigations to reach the public record.
- **1996 STS-80 Space Shuttle Columbia images.** NASA astronauts on the November–December
  1996 mission photographed a small triangular/cone-shaped object in low-Earth orbit.
- **2019 U.S. East Coast aviator debrief.** A military aviator with 28 years of service
  describes an object with flight characteristics "unlike anything I had seen" — small,
  fast, opposite-direction, tracked visually for ~10–15 seconds before the recorder was
  turned on; the object then accelerated out of frame during a zoom-in.
- **2020 Atlantic Ocean "jellyfish-shaped" range fouler.** A debrief of a training-range
  incursion describes an object resembling a large balloon but never positively identified.
- **2025 Yellow Sea "six-pointed star."** A U.S. Indo-Pacific Command sensor tracks "an
  area of contrast resembling a six-pointed star" near China.

The Debrief's editorial thrust, reflected in the headline and consistent with the outlet's
prior PURSUE coverage, is that transparency is measurably increasing — WAR.GOV/UFO has
already logged more than 1.7 billion cumulative hits since May 8, 2026 — but the *analytical*
disclosure gap is not closing at the same rate. Videos come out, but resolution, sensor
metadata, and contextual analysis remain limited; historical documents are opened, but the
underlying phenomena (green fireballs, orbital objects, jellyfish-shaped range foulers) are
still not conclusively explained. Pentagon officials continue to say UAP investigations
examine a range of possible explanations, including weather balloons, atmospheric
conditions, and advanced foreign technology, and have not established evidence of
extraterrestrial origin.

The Debrief closes by noting that Sean Parnell, Assistant to the Secretary of War for
Public Affairs, has confirmed that additional PURSUE tranches are already in preparation.

**五角大楼公布新一批 UAP 视频与历史档案，但答案依旧扑朔迷离**

据 The Debrief 报道，2026 年 7 月 10 日（周五），美国战争部（Department of War, DoW）
依据"总统 UAP 遭遇解封与报告系统（PURSUE）"公布了第四批不明异常现象（UAP）档案。这批
新材料为 WAR.GOV/UFO 档案库补充了新一批解密图像、历史文件以及全域异常处置办公室（AARO）
迄今未公开的视频；但在 The Debrief 看来，这一批发布并未真正推动公众对这些物体本质的理解。

The Debrief 梳理这批档案共 40 份，其中包括：14 份文档、19 段视频、4 条音频与 3 张图像，
来源涵盖战争部、美国国家航空航天局（NASA）、中央情报局（CIA）、联邦调查局（FBI）与
能源部（Department of Energy）。文章指出，绝大多数视频呈现的是通过美军战斗机、无人机
或民用航空器上的多传感器光电瞄准系统所拍下的小型、模糊物体；这些画面延续了 PURSUE 前
几批的风格：足以引发疑问，但分辨率与背景信息又不足以给出确定结论。

The Debrief 挑出几件突出物件：

- **1949 年洛斯阿拉莫斯"绿色火球"会议纪要。** 曼哈顿计划一代的科学家——包括爱德华·特勒
  （Edward Teller）、诺里斯·E·布拉德伯里（Norris E. Bradbury）以及流星专家林肯·拉帕兹
  （Lincoln LaPaz）——曾就 1948 年年末在新墨西哥州核武设施上空反复出现、水平掠过的
  亮绿色物体展开辩论。纪要中，LaPaz 谈到他本人于 1948 年 12 月 12 日目击到的那一次事件时
  说："绝对不是常规的陨石坠落。"The Debrief 认为，这是最早由美国政府正式立案、并如今
  进入公开档案的 UAP 调查之一。
- **1996 年 STS-80 哥伦比亚号航天飞机图像。** 1996 年 11 月–12 月，任务宇航员拍摄到近地
  轨道上一个小型三角形/圆锥形物体。
- **2019 年美国东岸飞行员汇报。** 一名服役 28 年的军方飞行员形容目击物体的飞行特性
  "不同于我此前所见的任何飞行器"——尺寸小、速度快、朝相反方向飞行；他先目视跟踪了约
  10–15 秒才打开录像机，随后放大画面时物体加速冲出视场。
- **2020 年大西洋"水母形"靶场闯入物。** 训练靶场遭闯入的汇报，把这枚外观类似大型气球、
  始终未被确认身份的物体形容为"水母状"。
- **2025 年黄海"六角星"。** 美军印太司令部的一部传感器在中国附近黄海上空跟踪到"一块
  形似六角星的对比度异常区域"。

The Debrief 的编辑立场——从标题以及该媒体此前对 PURSUE 的一贯报道路径皆可看出——是：
"透明度"确实在按可衡量的方式增加（WAR.GOV/UFO 自 2026 年 5 月 8 日上线以来累计访问量
已超过 17 亿次），但"分析层面的信息披露"并未同步跟上。视频不断放出来，但分辨率、传感
器元数据以及背景研判信息仍然有限；历史文件不断被打开，然而底层现象——绿色火球、轨道
物体、水母形靶场闯入物——依旧没有得到确定性的解释。五角大楼官员则继续强调，UAP 调查
覆盖包括气象气球、大气现象与他国先进技术在内的多种可能，并没有确立任何外星起源的证据。

文章结尾指出，战争部长公共事务助理肖恩·帕内尔（Sean Parnell）已确认下一批 PURSUE 档案
正在筹备之中。
