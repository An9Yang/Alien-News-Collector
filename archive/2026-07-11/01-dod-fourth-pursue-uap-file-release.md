**Department of War Publishes Fourth Release of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Files on WAR.GOV/UFO**

WASHINGTON — On July 10, 2026, the U.S. Department of War (DoW) published the fourth
official release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files
under the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE). The
new tranche is available at WAR.GOV/UFO and joins the three previous batches published on
May 8, May 22, and June 12, 2026.

"Today, the Department of War is publishing the fourth release of declassified and historical
Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and
Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE)," said Sean Parnell, Assistant to the
Secretary of War for Public Affairs and Chief Pentagon Spokesman. "The collection continues
to be housed on WAR.GOV/UFO, and the Department will release additional files on a rolling
basis. The Department of War and our agency partners are actively working on the next
release of UAP files."

**What the fourth batch contains**

The fourth PURSUE tranche comprises 40 files drawn from the Department of War, NASA, the
Central Intelligence Agency, the Federal Bureau of Investigation, and the Department of
Energy. By type, the release includes:

- 14 documents
- 19 videos
- 4 audio recordings
- 3 images

Several of the videos are previously unseen U.S. military recordings acquired by the
Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO). Most depict small, blurry objects
captured through multi-sensor electro-optical targeting systems on board fighter aircraft or
uncrewed aerial vehicles; a subset were recorded by imaging systems on board civilian
aircraft.

**Historical files: 1949 Los Alamos "green fireballs" conference**

Among the historical documents is the transcript of a February 1949 conference held at the
Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory to discuss a wave of unexplained "green fireballs" observed
across New Mexico and the American Southwest in late 1948 and early 1949. Participants
included Dr. Edward Teller, Dr. Norris E. Bradbury (then director of Los Alamos), and
meteor expert Dr. Lincoln LaPaz, along with representatives from the Atomic Energy
Commission and U.S. Air Force intelligence.

According to the newly released transcript, LaPaz told the conference that the December 12,
1948, event he had personally witnessed was "most certainly not a conventional meteorite
fall." He described a fireball that appeared "in full intensity instantly," maintained a
constant brightness, and travelled on a nearly horizontal path before breaking into "bright
green" fragments. Another attendee noted that "nothing like this ... has ever been observed
in the case of meteorite drops." No conventional explanation was reached at the meeting.

**Historical files: 1996 STS-80 Space Shuttle Columbia imagery**

A separate historical series in the release originates from NASA's STS-80 mission, flown by
Space Shuttle Columbia between November 19 and December 7, 1996. The PURSUE entry
publishes a set of three photographs taken by astronauts on that mission depicting a small
triangular or cone-shaped object in low-Earth orbit near the center of the frame.

**Recent case: 2019 Eastern U.S. military aviator encounter**

The release also publishes a debrief documenting a 2019 encounter over the eastern United
States, in which a military aviator and four other personnel observed and briefly video-taped
an object that could not be identified. The aviator wrote: "I noticed an object with flight
characteristics unlike anything I had seen in my 28 years of performing for the [Air Force]
and Navy. A small object was below us and appeared to be traveling in a straight line
opposite our direction at high speed. I tracked it for ~10-15 seconds before we turned on
the recorder to provide the attached video. When I zoomed in to try and achieve more
resolution, the object's speed took [it] out of my FOV and I was unable to reacquire, even at
a lower zoom."

**Recent case: 2020 Atlantic Ocean "jellyfish" object**

Another 2020 debrief from an Atlantic Ocean training range describes a "range fouler"
resembling a large balloon that investigators could not positively identify. The debrief
characterizes the object as "jellyfish-shaped."

**Recent case: 2025 Yellow Sea "six-pointed star"**

The most recent events described in the batch come from 2025, in the U.S. Indo-Pacific
Command area of responsibility. One video shows a military sensor tracking "an area of
contrast resembling a six-pointed star" over the Yellow Sea near China.

**Program context**

PURSUE was established by presidential direction earlier in 2026 as a multi-agency effort to
find, review, identify, declassify, and publicly release unresolved UAP-related records and
historical documents held across the federal government. The initiative is led by the
Department of War in coordination with AARO and other agencies. Cumulative traffic to
WAR.GOV/UFO has exceeded 1.7 billion hits since the site launched on May 8, 2026.

The Department of War has not established a specific date for the fifth release but has
stated that additional files are actively being prepared.

**美国战争部在 WAR.GOV/UFO 发布第四批不明异常现象（UAP）文件**

华盛顿——2026 年 7 月 10 日，美国战争部（Department of War, DoW）依据"总统 UAP 遭遇解封与
报告系统（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE）"正式
公布第四批解密与历史性不明异常现象（UAP）档案。新的一批文件在 WAR.GOV/UFO 上线，此前
2026 年 5 月 8 日、5 月 22 日与 6 月 12 日已分别发布过前三批。

"今天，战争部依据 PURSUE 计划公布第四批解密与历史性 UAP 文件，"战争部长公共事务助理暨
五角大楼首席发言人肖恩·帕内尔（Sean Parnell）表示，"档案集将继续存放于 WAR.GOV/UFO，
本部会继续滚动发布新的文件。战争部与合作机构正在积极准备下一批 UAP 材料。"

**第四批的具体内容**

第四批 PURSUE 档案共 40 份文件，来自战争部、美国国家航空航天局（NASA）、中央情报局
（CIA）、联邦调查局（FBI）与能源部（DoE）。按类型划分：

- 文档 14 份
- 视频 19 段
- 音频录音 4 条
- 图像 3 张

其中若干视频为战争部下属"全域异常处置办公室（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office,
AARO）"此前未公开的军方影像。多数画面显示的是通过战斗机或无人机上的多传感器光电瞄准
系统所捕获的小型、模糊物体；也有一部分来自民用航空器上的成像系统。

**历史档案：1949 年洛斯阿拉莫斯"绿色火球"会议**

历史档案中最引人注意的一份，是 1949 年 2 月在洛斯阿拉莫斯科学实验室（Los Alamos
Scientific Laboratory）召开的一次内部会议记录。会议专门讨论 1948 年末至 1949 年初在新墨西
哥州及美国西南部反复出现的"绿色火球（green fireballs）"现象。与会者包括爱德华·特勒
（Edward Teller）、时任洛斯阿拉莫斯所长诺里斯·E·布拉德伯里（Norris E. Bradbury）、以
及流星专家林肯·拉帕兹（Lincoln LaPaz），此外还有原子能委员会（AEC）与美国空军情报部门
的代表。

根据新公布的会议纪要，LaPaz 在会上告诉与会者，他本人于 1948 年 12 月 12 日亲眼所见的那一
次事件"绝非常规陨石坠落"。他描述这枚火球"瞬间达到全亮度"、亮度在整个可见过程中保持恒定，
沿几乎水平的轨迹飞行，最后碎裂成"明亮的绿色"碎片。另一位与会者则指出，"从未有陨石坠落
事件出现过与此类似的情形"。会议最终未能就该现象给出任何常规解释。

**历史档案：1996 年 STS-80 哥伦比亚号航天飞机影像**

另一组历史材料来自 NASA 的 STS-80 任务——1996 年 11 月 19 日至 12 月 7 日由哥伦比亚号
航天飞机（Space Shuttle Columbia）执行。PURSUE 此次发布了该次任务宇航员拍摄的三张照片，
画面中央附近可见一枚在近地轨道上的小型三角形或圆锥形物体。

**近期案例：2019 年美国东部军方飞行员遭遇**

档案还包括一份 2019 年在美国东部空域发生的遭遇事件汇报，报告称一名军方飞行员与另外四名
机组人员共同目击并短暂拍摄了一枚无法辨认的物体。这名飞行员写道：

"我注意到一枚飞行特性与我在（空军）与海军服役 28 年间所见任何飞行器都不相同的物体。
它位于我们下方，看似沿一条直线以极高速度朝我们相反方向飞行。我跟踪了大约 10 到 15 秒
才打开录像机，拍下了附件中的这段视频。当我放大镜头以获得更高分辨率时，那个物体的速度
使其飞出了我的视场（FOV），即便再拉低倍数也无法重新捕捉到它。"

**近期案例：2020 年大西洋"水母形"物体**

另一份 2020 年的汇报来自大西洋上的一处训练靶场，其中记录了一枚被称为"靶场干扰物（range
fouler）"的物体，其外观类似大型气球，但调查人员始终无法确证其身份。汇报把这一物体形容
为"水母状（jellyfish-shaped）"。

**近期案例：2025 年黄海"六角星"**

本次公布中时间最近的事件发生于 2025 年，位于美军印太司令部辖区。其中一段视频显示某型军
用传感器正在中国附近的黄海海域上空跟踪"一片形似六角星的对比度异常区域"。

**计划背景**

PURSUE 由总统于 2026 年年初签署行政令后正式启动，是由多个联邦机构参与的联合行动，目
的是查找、审查、鉴别、解密并公开美国政府各部门持有的未结案 UAP 相关档案与历史文件。计
划由战争部牵头，与 AARO 及其他机构协调实施。自 2026 年 5 月 8 日站点上线至今，
WAR.GOV/UFO 的累计访问量已超过 17 亿次。

战争部尚未公布第五批文件的具体发布日期，但表示相关材料正在积极准备之中。
