## Original (English)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — July 10, 2026**

*Office of the Assistant to the Secretary of War for Public Affairs, U.S. Department of War*

Today, the Department of War is publishing the fourth release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE).

At the direction of President Donald J. Trump, the Department of War is overseeing a multiagency effort to expeditiously find, review, identify, declassify and publicly release unresolved Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena-related records and historical documents in the federal government's possession.

"Today, the Department of War is publishing the fourth release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE)," said Assistant to the Secretary of War for Public Affairs and Chief Pentagon Spokesman Sean Parnell.

The fourth tranche includes previously unseen U.S. military videos depicting unidentified aerial objects acquired by the Department of War's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO). It also incorporates several historical documents dating back to the 1940s, the majority of which had previously been made available in past releases by various U.S. government agencies.

"The collection continues to be housed on WAR.GOV/UFO, and the Department will release additional files on a rolling basis," Parnell said.

The Department of War and its agency partners are actively working on the next release of UAP files, which can be expected to appear on the DOW website in the coming weeks.

WAR.GOV/UFO was launched on May 8, 2026. Since its launch, the site has received more than 1.7 billion hits worldwide.

## 简体中文译稿

**即时发布 — 2026 年 7 月 10 日**

*美国战争部 主管公共事务的战争部部长助理办公室（Office of the Assistant to the Secretary of War for Public Affairs, U.S. Department of War）*

美国战争部（Department of War）今日在"总统 UAP 事件解封与报告系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，PURSUE）框架下，发布第四批已解密与历史性的"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena，UAP）档案。

在美国总统唐纳德·J·特朗普（Donald J. Trump）的指示下，战争部正牵头多机构协同行动，加快寻找、审查、鉴别、解密并公开发布联邦政府所掌握的、尚未解释的 UAP 相关记录与历史文件。

"今天，战争部正在发布 PURSUE 框架下第四批已解密与历史性的 UAP 档案，"主管公共事务的战争部部长助理、首席五角大楼发言人肖恩·帕内尔（Sean Parnell）表示。

第四批文件包括战争部下属"全域异常解决办公室"（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office，AARO）获得的、此前从未公开的美国军方视频，视频显示了不明空中物体。此外还包括数份最早可追溯到 1940 年代的历史文件，其中多数曾由不同美国政府机构以往批次公开过。

"该资料集将继续存放于 WAR.GOV/UFO 网站，战争部将以滚动方式陆续发布更多文件，"帕内尔说。

战争部及其合作机构目前正在准备下一批 UAP 档案的发布，预计将于未来数周内上线战争部官网。

WAR.GOV/UFO 于 2026 年 5 月 8 日上线，自上线以来，该站点在全球范围内已累计获得逾 17 亿次访问。
