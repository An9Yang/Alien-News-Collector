**IMMEDIATE RELEASE — September 14, 2026**
**U.S. Department of War, Washington, D.C.**

In direct alignment with President Donald J. Trump's mandate for comprehensive
transparency on Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), the Department of War
today announced the issuance of a targeted legal waiver that establishes an
authorized, legally protected disclosure mechanism for current and former
personnel to provide UAP‑related National Defense Information (NDI) to
designated representatives of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System
for UAP Encounters (PURSUE).

Under this directive, all current and former Department of War service
members, civilian personnel, and contractors holding or previously granted
access to UAP‑related National Defense Information (NDI) are authorized to
disclose protected information directly to official PURSUE representatives.

The waiver supersedes civil and administrative enforcement provisions
contained within Non‑Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Special Access Program
Indoctrination Agreements (SAPIAs) previously executed within the United
States, strictly for communications directed to the PURSUE team. The
authorization applies only to disclosures made through the designated PURSUE
channel; it does not authorize public disclosure, communications with parties
outside the PURSUE effort, declassification of UAP information, or public
discussion of classified programs by covered personnel.

The Department described the measure as "targeted legal relief" designed to
remove longstanding barriers cited by insiders. Historically, individuals with
direct knowledge of UAP‑related programs cited concerns regarding potential
legal liability, loss of clearances, or administrative prosecution stemming
from standard NDAs and SAP indoctrination terms. This waiver removes those
barriers by explicitly authorizing covered disclosures to PURSUE
representatives without triggering breach‑of‑agreement penalties or standard
NDA violations.

By opening this protected pathway, the Department said, PURSUE will be able
to establish an authorized pipeline for systematic review, security
evaluation, and potential declassification of historical and ongoing
UAP‑related data in furtherance of national mission objectives. The
Department of War added that the waiver is consistent with President Trump's
February 2026 directive to identify and release records connected to UFOs
and extraterrestrial life, and with the ongoing rolling publication of
declassified files on WAR.GOV/UFO, which has drawn over 1.7 billion visits
worldwide since its May 8, 2026 launch.

The Department noted that covered disclosures should be routed through the
official PURSUE contact channel identified on WAR.GOV/UFO, and that current
personnel should coordinate through their chain of command and security
managers where required. The Department of War will continue to work with
the interagency PURSUE task force, the All‑domain Anomaly Resolution Office
(AARO), the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), and
other partners to review, evaluate, and release qualifying UAP materials on
a rolling basis.

*— U.S. Department of War, Office of the Assistant to the Secretary of War
for Public Affairs*

**即时发布 · 2026 年 9 月 14 日**
**美国战争部，华盛顿特区**

为切实贯彻美国总统唐纳德·J·特朗普（Donald J. Trump）关于全面透明化处理
"不明异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena，UAP）事务的指令，战争部
（Department of War）今日宣布签发一项**有针对性的法律豁免**（targeted legal
waiver），为在职及退役人员向"总统解密与 UAP 事件报送系统"（Presidential
Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，PURSUE）的授权代表提供
UAP 相关**国防机密信息**（National Defense Information，NDI）建立一条获得
法律保护的正式披露渠道。

依据本指令，战争部所有**现役与退役军人、文职雇员及承包商**，只要曾持有或
仍持有 UAP 相关国防机密信息（NDI）的知情权限，即被授权将受保护的信息
**直接**披露给 PURSUE 的官方代表。

该豁免**优先适用于**（supersedes）以往在美国境内签署的**保密协议**
（Non-Disclosure Agreements，NDAs）以及**特殊接触计划知情同意书**
（Special Access Program Indoctrination Agreements，SAPIAs）中所包含的
**民事及行政执法条款**，但**仅限于**向 PURSUE 团队进行的沟通。该授权
**只适用于**通过 PURSUE 指定渠道进行的披露；它不授权任何**对外公开披露**、
不授权与 PURSUE 之外任何一方的沟通，也**不构成对 UAP 信息的解密**，更不允许
被覆盖人员公开谈论其他机密项目。

战争部将该措施描述为"有针对性的法律救济"（targeted legal relief），旨在
拆除内部知情者长期以来所指出的障碍。历史上，直接了解 UAP 相关项目的
人员曾担心：如披露相关信息，可能面临源自标准 NDA 及 SAP 知情同意条款的
**法律责任、失去安保许可（clearance），或遭到行政追诉**。此次豁免通过
明确授权面向 PURSUE 代表的相关披露，使当事人不再因此触发违约罚则或
标准 NDA 违规。

战争部表示，开辟这一受保护的通道之后，PURSUE 将能够建立一条正式的
**授权通道**（authorized pipeline），对历史及在办的 UAP 相关数据进行
**系统性审阅、安全评估与潜在的解密**，服务于国家任务目标。战争部并指出，
本次豁免与特朗普总统 2026 年 2 月发出的"识别并公开与 UFO 及外星生命有关
档案"指令一脉相承，也与 WAR.GOV/UFO 网站按批次滚动公布的解密档案同步——
该网站自 2026 年 5 月 8 日上线以来，全球访问量已超 17 亿次。

战争部并要求：所受覆盖的披露，应通过 WAR.GOV/UFO 上公布的 PURSUE 官方
联络渠道进行；对在职人员而言，应视需要通过其指挥链及安保主管协调办理。
战争部将继续与跨机构的 PURSUE 工作组、**全域异常处置办公室**（All-domain
Anomaly Resolution Office，AARO）、**国家情报总监办公室**（Office of the
Director of National Intelligence，ODNI）及其他伙伴合作，按批次审阅、评估
并释出符合条件的 UAP 相关材料。

*——美国战争部 · 战争部长公共事务助理办公室*
