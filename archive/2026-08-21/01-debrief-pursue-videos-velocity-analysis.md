**Headline:** Scientists Just Analyzed More Than 100 UAP Videos Released by the Pentagon's 'PURSUE' Program—This is What They Learned

**Standfirst:** A new analysis of 112 videos released so far under the Pentagon's Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE) reveals a foundational problem for anyone hoping to prove the sensor footage captures anomalous flight characteristics: without the sensor telemetry that has been redacted or withheld, the raw imagery cannot, on its own, demonstrate extraordinary velocities.

Researchers Jacob Haqq-Misra of Blue Marble Space and Ravi Kopparapu of NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center examined 112 videos that the U.S. Department of War (formerly the Department of Defense) has released since May 8, 2026 under PURSUE. The study — described by The Debrief as among the first to test the corpus systematically — has not yet undergone peer review.

**The core finding.** Haqq-Misra and Kopparapu report that the apparent speed of an object suggested by its movement across a camera's field of view is not necessarily an accurate indication of its real physical velocity. Converting pixel motion into a true velocity requires four inputs at minimum: the range from the camera to the object, the velocity of the observing aircraft or platform, the aspect angle between the object's flight path and the observer, and the field-of-view angle of the sensor. According to their analysis, no sensor video in the PURSUE corpus provides that complete set of information.

For the one case where enough was recoverable to bound the target's real velocity, the authors report only an upper bound of roughly Mach 0.4 — decidedly subsonic, and far from anything that could be described as extraordinary.

**On the redactions.** "Knowing the distance from the camera to the object is essential," Haqq-Misra is quoted as saying. He notes that the missing data may lie beneath the redacted black boxes visible in many of the released clips, and that unredacted versions would be far more useful for independent physical analysis.

**On public interpretation.** Haqq-Misra cautions that the conclusions "may not be fully appreciated in the broader public" even though they would come as no surprise to people in the aviation, aerospace, and intelligence communities already following the disclosure process. He urges readers to avoid both extraordinary conclusions and outright dismissal when interpreting PURSUE releases.

**What comes next.** The authors say one goal of the UAP Science Advisory Council is to recommend what kinds of metadata future PURSUE tranches should include so that independent scientists can conduct meaningful kinematic analyses. Doing so, they argue, could resolve cases that are currently stuck in the "unresolved" column simply for lack of context data.

**Corpus context.** Since PURSUE launched on May 8, 2026, the Department of War has published five tranches of UAP files (on May 8, May 22, June 12, July 10, and August 7), releasing more than 100 videos alongside documents, images, and historical records from the Pentagon, FBI, CIA, State Department, and Executive Office of the President. AARO's own fiscal-year 2025 annual report, released in July 2026, counted 319 newly received or previously unreported cases, resolved 114 of them, and placed 191 in the Active Archive; nine warrant deeper analysis and three await closure approval. AARO has said it has found no verifiable evidence that any of the cases it has examined involved extraterrestrial beings or technology.

The preprint underpinning the Debrief report is available on arXiv as "Limits on Velocity Recovery from the PURSUE Sensor Videos" (arXiv:2608.12445).

**标题：** 科学家分析五角大楼 “PURSUE” 计划公开的百余段 UAP 视频，得出的结论令人失望

**导语：** 一项对 “PURSUE”（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters，总统主导的 UAP 遭遇解密与报送体系）迄今公开的 112 段视频的新分析揭示了一个根本性问题：任何人若想通过这些镜头证明所拍到的物体具有异常飞行性能，都会在传感器遥测数据缺失或被涂黑（redacted）之后陷入僵局——单靠画面本身，不足以证明目标具有异常速度。

蓝色大理石太空（Blue Marble Space）的雅各布·哈克-米斯拉（Jacob Haqq-Misra）与 NASA 戈达德太空飞行中心（NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center）的拉维·科帕拉普（Ravi Kopparapu）系统检视了美国国防部（现称 U.S. Department of War，战争部）自 2026 年 5 月 8 日起在 PURSUE 框架下陆续释出的 112 段视频。The Debrief 称这是首批对该视频语料库进行系统性检验的研究之一。该研究尚未经过同行评议。

**核心结论。** 哈克-米斯拉与科帕拉普指出，一个物体在相机视场中横越所呈现出的“表观速度”，未必反映其真实物理速度。若要将像素位移换算成真实速度，至少需要四类输入：相机到目标的距离（range）、观测平台自身的速度、目标飞行路径相对于观测者的姿态角（aspect angle），以及传感器的视场角（field-of-view）。他们发现，在 PURSUE 语料库中，没有任何一段传感器视频同时提供了这全部四项要素。

在唯一一个可以恢复出真实速度上限的案例里，作者只能给出约 0.4 马赫（Mach 0.4）的上界——明显低于音速，谈不上任何“非常规”表现。

**关于遮蔽处理。** “知道相机到目标的距离是关键，”哈克-米斯拉说。他指出，缺失的数据很可能就藏在许多视频里那些被涂黑的框（redacted black boxes）之下；如果能拿到未涂黑的版本，独立物理分析将大为受益。

**关于公众解读。** 哈克-米斯拉提醒，这些结论“未必能被更广泛的公众充分理解”，尽管对航空、航天与情报界持续关注解密进程的人来说并不意外。他呼吁读者在解读 PURSUE 视频时既不要跳到超常结论，也不要一概否定。

**下一步。** 作者说，UAP 科学咨询委员会（UAP Science Advisory Council）的目标之一，就是就未来 PURSUE 释出中应附带哪些元数据向政府提出建议，以便独立科学家可以完成有意义的运动学（kinematic）分析。他们认为，这样做可以让一些目前仅因缺乏上下文数据而停留在“未定”栏目的案例得到解决。

**语料背景。** 自 2026 年 5 月 8 日 PURSUE 启动以来，战争部（Department of War）已完成五轮 UAP 档案发布（2026 年 5 月 8 日、5 月 22 日、6 月 12 日、7 月 10 日、8 月 7 日），累计公开百余段视频，以及来自五角大楼、联邦调查局（FBI）、中央情报局（CIA）、国务院（State Department）和总统行政办公室（Executive Office of the President）的文件、图像与历史档案。全域异常解析办公室（AARO，All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office）2026 年 7 月发布的 2025 财年年度报告显示，本报告期新收到或此前未上报的案件共 319 宗，已了结 114 宗，191 宗归入“活跃档案”（Active Archive），另有 9 宗值得进一步分析、3 宗待批准结案。AARO 表示，其审查过的案件中未发现任何可证实的外星生命或技术证据。

The Debrief 报道所依据的预印本发表在 arXiv，题为《Limits on Velocity Recovery from the PURSUE Sensor Videos》（arXiv:2608.12445）。
