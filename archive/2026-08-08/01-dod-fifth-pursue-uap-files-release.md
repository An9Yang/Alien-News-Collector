## Original (English)

**WASHINGTON — Aug. 7, 2026.** The U.S. Department of War today published the fifth
release of declassified and historical Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files
under the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE),
adding 41 new documents, images, videos and audio recordings to the growing public
archive hosted at **war.gov/ufo**.

PURSUE is the multi-agency effort directed by President Donald J. Trump through the
Department of War to expeditiously find, review, identify, declassify and publicly
release unresolved UAP-related records and historical documents in the federal
government's possession. The initiative began with a first release on May 8, 2026,
and Friday's tranche is the fifth batch since then.

The 41 new files were drawn from the Pentagon, the Federal Bureau of Investigation,
the Central Intelligence Agency, the Department of State and the Executive Office of
the President. The oldest record in the release dates to 1950; the most recent
material is from 2026. The batch contains scanned copies of memos from the 1950s,
CIA notes from the 1960s, State Department cables, FBI witness-interview records,
digital and artistic renderings intended to illustrate what witnesses reported, and
military-sensor video and audio.

**Notable incidents in the release**

- **Afghanistan, 2002 — "equilateral triangle" over Bagram.** In an FBI interview
  record, a former U.S. military pilot recalled that at approximately 4:30 a.m.
  local time the stars over Bagram Air Base "were getting blacked out"; it appeared
  as if something huge went overhead from west to east, in an equilateral-triangle
  shape, moving silently at a consistent altitude and roughly 150 knots with no
  visible lights. The pilot estimated the object at about 500 feet across but said
  he could not fully make out its shape.
- **Gulf of Oman, September 2021 — AC-130J "cold-orb" swarm.** U.S. Special
  Operations personnel aboard an AC-130J gunship during an exercise reported
  roughly 25 UAP encounters involving objects described in an intelligence report
  as "cold orbs" moving at estimated speeds of 250 mph to 1,300 mph. The objects
  were reported to have flown in formations, maneuvered aggressively, and appeared
  to react after the aircraft fired its cannon.
- **U.S. Central Command video, 2025.** CENTCOM submitted a report to the
  All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of 1 minute 39 seconds
  of video footage from an electro-optical and infrared sensor aboard a U.S.
  military platform.
- **Colorado, October 2023 — silent translucent triangle.** An FBI account
  describes a former Pentagon employee who reported observing a nearly
  translucent triangular object over his Colorado home; the witness said the
  object was completely silent and may have passed as low as 300 feet above the
  ground.
- **Brazil, 1963 — State Department cable.** Documents include a State Department
  cable discussing a local news report of a large "metal balloon" that crashed,
  with a dead crew member said to be wearing a spacesuit bearing strange markings.

**Statement from the department**

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said additional disclosures are already being
prepared. "The Department will release additional files on a rolling basis," Parnell
said, adding that the department and other federal agencies are working on the next
release.

Since war.gov/ufo launched on May 8, 2026, the department says the site has received
more than 1.7 billion page views worldwide. All previously released PURSUE batches
and the newest fifth-release material can be accessed there.

## 简体中文译稿

**华盛顿 · 2026 年 8 月 7 日。** 美国战争部（U.S. Department of War）今日通过"总统 UAP 遭遇解封与
报告系统"（Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters, PURSUE），发布
第五批已解密、具历史意义的"未识别异常现象"（Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, UAP）档案，共
计 41 份新文档、图像、视频与音频，全部上传至 **war.gov/ufo**。

PURSUE 是总统唐纳德·J·特朗普通过战争部下达指令的跨机构行动，负责快速查找、审阅、
识别、解密并公开联邦政府尚未定案的 UAP 相关档案与历史文件。该行动于 2026 年 5 月 8 日
首次公开发布档案，周五的新批次是自那时起的第五批。

新公开的 41 份档案来源于五角大楼（Pentagon）、联邦调查局（FBI）、中央情报局（CIA）、
国务院（Department of State）以及总统行政办公室（Executive Office of the President）。
最古老的一份可追溯至 1950 年，最新一份则来自 2026 年。批次内包括 1950 年代备忘录扫描件、
1960 年代 CIA 笔记、国务院电报、FBI 目击者笔录，以及试图还原目击者所描述景象的
数字化与手绘示意图，另有军用传感器采集的视频与音频。

**本批次值得关注的案例**

- **阿富汗，2002 年——巴格拉姆基地上空的"等边三角形"。** 在一份 FBI 访谈笔录中，
  一名前美军飞行员回忆，当地时间凌晨约 4:30，巴格拉姆空军基地（Bagram）上空的星光
  "开始被遮蔽"，仿佛有巨大的物体自西向东从头顶掠过，呈等边三角形，安静地以稳定
  高度、约 150 节的速度飞行，未见任何灯光。飞行员估计该物体宽约 500 英尺，但称
  无法完整辨识其形状。
- **阿曼湾，2021 年 9 月——AC-130J"冷球"群。** 美军特种作战人员在一次演习中乘坐
  AC-130J 炮艇机时，报告了约 25 起 UAP 遭遇，情报报告将相关物体描述为"冷球"
  （cold orbs），估计速度为每小时 250 至 1,300 英里。这些物体据称成编队飞行、进行
  激烈机动，并在炮艇机开炮后似乎作出反应。
- **美中央司令部视频，2025 年。** 美国中央司令部（U.S. Central Command, CENTCOM）向
  "全域异常解决办公室"（All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, AARO）提交了一份
  1 分 39 秒的视频报告，画面由某美军平台上的光电与红外传感器采集。
- **科罗拉多州，2023 年 10 月——沉默的半透明三角形。** 一份 FBI 记录描述一名前五角大楼
  雇员在其科罗拉多州住所上空观察到一枚近乎透明的三角形物体，目击者称该物体完全无声，
  可能低至距地面 300 英尺掠过。
- **巴西，1963 年——国务院电报。** 档案中包括一份国务院电报，其中援引当地新闻报道，
  描述一枚大型"金属气球"坠毁，一名遇难乘员身着带有奇怪标记的太空服。

**战争部表态**

五角大楼发言人肖恩·帕内尔（Sean Parnell）表示，更多公开正在筹备中。他称："战争部将
以滚动方式陆续公布更多档案。" 他补充说，战争部与其他联邦机构正在推进下一批的准备工作。

war.gov/ufo 网站自 2026 年 5 月 8 日上线以来，据战争部披露，全球访问量已超过 17 亿次。
此前四批 PURSUE 档案以及此次最新的第五批材料均可在该网站查阅。
